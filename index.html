Folio Scholar

A reference manager for your Mac

SupportPrivacy Policy

Folio keeps your papers, books and notes in one searchable library on your Mac. It files PDFs by author, year and title, fills in details from the scholarly databases, finds duplicates, follows references and citations, and exports citations and BibTeX.

Get help or report a problem

Open an issue at github.com/kit-matan/folio-app-site/issues. Please include your macOS version, Folio's version (Folio › About Folio) and what you were doing when the problem happened. A free GitHub account is needed to post.

Please don't attach papers or other files you're not allowed to share.

Getting started

  • On first launch, choose the folder that holds your papers. Folio indexes the PDFs there and files the papers you add inside that folder.
  • Already used Folio outside the App Store? Choose Bring Over a Folio Library… on the welcome screen to copy your library, notes and search index.
  • The full user guide is in the app under Help › Folio User Guide.

Common questions

Does Folio upload my papers? No. Everything stays on your Mac. See the privacy policy.

Why does Folio ask for access to a folder? macOS lets apps read only the folders you choose. Folio asks once and remembers your choice.

Can I undo a merge or an automatic match? Yes. Merges can be undone in Find Duplicates, and batch matches from the Unmatched view.
