Privacy Policy

Folio Scholar for Mac

SupportPrivacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

Folio is a reference manager that runs entirely on your Mac.

We collect no data. Folio has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your papers, notes, highlights, lists and settings are stored only on your Mac, in Folio's own storage and in the folder you choose for your papers.

Online services you use through Folio. When you search the web for papers, match a paper to an online record, or look up related papers, Folio sends your search text, the paper's title, or its DOI directly to the public scholarly databases OpenAlex, Crossref or arXiv, and downloads open-access PDFs from the address they give. These requests go from your Mac to those services; they do not pass through us. If you enter an OpenAlex API key, it is stored on your Mac and sent only to OpenAlex. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.

Files. Folio reads and writes only the files and folders you choose (your papers folder, PDFs you add, .bib files you link, and, if you turn on the watch folder, your Downloads folder).

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact. Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/kit-matan/folio-app-site/issues.
